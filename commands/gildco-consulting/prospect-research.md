# GILDCO Prospect Research

Research and analyze this prospect for outreach: $ARGUMENTS

## Research Steps
1. Search for company information: size, sector, recent news, leadership team
2. Identify potential organizational pain points based on their industry
3. Map how GILDCO's 5-phase model applies to their situation
4. Find the right contact person (decision maker for operations/HR/transformation)

## Analysis Output
Create a prospect brief in `prospects/{company-name}-brief.md`:

### Company Profile
- Name, sector, approximate size
- Key leadership
- Recent news or strategic moves

### Pain Point Hypothesis
- 3 likely organizational inefficiencies based on their sector
- For each: which GILDCO phase addresses it

### Outreach Strategy
- Recommended contact person and channel (email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp)
- Personalized opening hook (reference specific company context)
- Value proposition angle most relevant to them

### Qualification Score
Rate 1-5 on:
- Budget likelihood (PYME vs enterprise)
- Pain point urgency
- Decision-maker accessibility
- Sector fit (hospitality premium = highest)
- Total /20

## Language
- Brief: Spanish
- Tone: internal document, direct and analytical
