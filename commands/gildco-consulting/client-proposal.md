# GILDCO Client Diagnosis Proposal

Create a client diagnosis proposal for: $ARGUMENTS

## Context
GILDCO sells measurable organizational capacity improvement through a 5-phase model:
1. **Human Intelligence** — deep talent radiography (people, climate, processes, times, leadership, tools, productivity)
2. **Efficiency Map** — where capacity is lost (area X loses $Y/hours/opportunities due to cause Z)
3. **Augmentation Plan** — what to change and expected ROI
4. **Implementation** — deploy technology, AI, automation, or operational changes
5. **Continuous Intelligence** — measure results and restart cycle

The diagnosis (Phase 1) is the entry product: fixed-price engagement delivering an Organizational Health Score + bottleneck cards + prioritized plan.

## Process
1. Research the client's industry using $ARGUMENTS (company name, sector, size, known pain points)
2. Draft a proposal in Spanish covering:
   - Personalized opening referencing their specific challenges
   - What GILDCO's diagnosis would uncover for THEIR organization
   - 3 hypothetical bottleneck examples relevant to their industry
   - Deliverables: Organizational Health Score, bottleneck cards with evidence, prioritized action plan
   - Timeline and next steps
3. Tone: professional but warm, data-driven, not salesy

## Bottleneck Card Format
Each hypothetical example should follow this structure:
- Human evidence + Operational evidence
- Root cause
- Proposed intervention
- Target metric
- Recovered capacity estimate
- Projected ROI

## Output
- Save as `proposals/{client-name}-proposal.md`
- Language: Spanish (Colombian professional)
- Length: 1.5-2 pages equivalent
- Include a one-paragraph email draft to send with the proposal
